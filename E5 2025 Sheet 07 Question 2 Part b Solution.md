Welcome back to class! Grab your seat. In the first part, we looked at forces; now we are going to look at **Conservation Laws**.

Think of this part of physics as **"Cosmic Accounting."** When a reaction happens, nature is very strict about keeping the books balanced. If you start with 5 units of "something," you usually better end with 5 units of that "something." If the numbers don't add up, the reaction is strictly forbidden (or "not conserved").

We are going to audit these four reactions to see if nature's books balance.

### The Accounting Ledger (Definitions)

Before we check the specific reactions, here are the three numbers we need to count for every particle:

1. **Electric Charge ($Q$):** We count protons as $+1$, electrons as $-1$, etc.
    
2. **Baryon Number ($B$):**
    
    - Are you a Proton or Neutron? Your score is **$+1$**.
        
    - Are you an Antiparticle ($\bar{p}$)? Your score is **$-1$**.
        
    - Are you anything else (electron, photon, meson/pion)? Your score is **$0$**.
        
3. **Lepton Number ($L$):**
    
    - Are you an Electron ($e^-$) or Neutrino ($\nu$)? Your score is **$+1$**.
        
    - Are you an Antiparticle ($e^+$ or $\bar{\nu}$)? Your score is **$-1$**.
        
    - Are you a Proton, Neutron, or Pion? Your score is **$0$**.

### Step-by-Step Audit

Let's check the balance sheets for each reaction in your matrix.

#### 1. Reaction: $\alpha + ^9\text{Be} \rightarrow ^{12}\text{C} + p$

- **The Audit (Electric Charge):**
    
    - **Left Side:**
        
        - $\alpha$ particle (which is a Helium nucleus): It has 2 protons. Charge = **$+2$**.
            
        - $^9\text{Be}$ (Beryllium): Look at the periodic table. Atomic number 4. Charge = **$+4$**.
            
        - **Total Left:** $2 + 4 = \mathbf{+6}$.
            
    - **Right Side:**
        
        - $^{12}\text{C}$ (Carbon): Atomic number 6. Charge = **$+6$**.
            
        - $p$ (Proton): Charge = **$+1$**.
            
        - **Total Right:** $6 + 1 = \mathbf{+7}$.
            
- **The Verdict:** Wait a minute! You started with +6 charge and ended with +7? You created electric charge out of nowhere! That is illegal in our universe.
    
- **Matrix Answer:** **Electric charge** is NOT conserved.
    

#### 2. Reaction: $^1n + ^{235}U \rightarrow ^{92}\text{Kr} + ^{142}\text{Ba} + 2^1n$

- **The Audit:**
    
    - This is classic nuclear fission (like in a nuclear power plant).
        
    - **Charge:** Uranium (92) vs Krypton (36) + Barium (56). $92 = 36 + 56$. Balanced.
        
    - **Baryons:** $1 + 235 = 236$. Right side: $92 + 142 + 2(1) = 236$. Balanced.
        
    - **Leptons:** None involved on either side. Balanced.
        
- **The Verdict:** Everything balances perfectly.
    
- **Matrix Answer:** **All conserved**.
    

#### 3. Reaction: $^1n \rightarrow \pi^+ + \pi^-$

- **The Audit (Baryon Number):**
    
    - **Left Side:** Neutron ($n$). It is a Baryon. Score = **$1$**.
        
    - **Right Side:** Pions ($\pi$). Pions are Mesons, not Baryons. Score = **$0$**.
        
- **The Verdict:** You started with 1 Baryon and ended with 0. You made a Baryon disappear into thin air! This violates Baryon number conservation.
    
- **Matrix Answer:** **Baryon number** is NOT conserved.
    

#### 4. Reaction: $p \rightarrow ^1n + e^+ + \bar{\nu}_e$

- **The Audit (Lepton Number):**
    
    - **Left Side:** Proton ($p$). It's a Baryon, not a lepton. Score = **$0$**.
        
    - **Right Side:**
        
        - Neutron ($n$): Score = $0$.
            
        - Positron ($e^+$): This is an anti-lepton. Score = **$-1$**.
            
        - Electron _Anti_-neutrino ($\bar{\nu}_e$): This is also an anti-lepton. Score = **$-1$**.
            
        - **Total Right:** $0 + (-1) + (-1) = \mathbf{-2}$.
            
- **The Verdict:** The left side has 0 lepton number, but the right side has -2. (Note: In real physics, usually a positron comes with a regular neutrino to balance $-1$ and $+1$. But this specific equation wrote an _anti_-neutrino, which breaks the law!).
    
- **Matrix Answer:** **Lepton number** is NOT conserved.

### The Follow-Up Question

**"Name at least two additional non-conserved quantities for the last process!"**

The professor's answer key lists **Energy** and **Parity**. Here is why:

1. **Energy (Mass):**
    
    - Think of mass as money. To make a reaction happen, you need to be able to "afford" the products.
        
    - **Left Side:** A Proton. Mass $\approx$ 938.3 MeV.
        
    - **Right Side:** A Neutron. Mass $\approx$ 939.6 MeV.
        
    - **The Problem:** The Neutron is heavier than the Proton! You cannot turn a lighter particle into a heavier particle (plus a positron) unless you add extra energy from the outside. A free proton standing still can never decay into a neutron because it violates **Conservation of Energy**.
        
2. **Parity:**
    
    - Parity is a fancy physics term for "mirror symmetry." If you watch a reaction in a mirror, it should look the same as in real life.
        
    - However, this reaction involves the **Weak Force** (we know this because of the neutrino!).
        
    - **The Rule:** The Weak Force is the "vampire" of physics—it has no reflection. It is the only force that violates Parity conservation.
