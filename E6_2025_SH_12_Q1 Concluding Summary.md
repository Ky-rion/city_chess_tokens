### 🎓 Exam Cheat Sheet: Absorption & Excitons

#### 1. Fundamental Energy Relations

- **Photon Energy ($E$):**
    
    $$E = h\nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$
    
    - **Practical Shortcut:** $E [\text{eV}] \approx \frac{1240}{\lambda [\text{nm}]}$
        
    - _Why you need this:_ If the exam gives you wavelength ($\text{nm}$) instead of eV, use this to convert quickly.
        

#### 2. The Band Gap ($E_g$)

- **Definition:** The energy difference between the top of the Valence Band ($E_V$) and the bottom of the Conduction Band ($E_C$).
    
    $$E_g = E_C - E_V$$
    
- **Condition for Continuum Absorption:**
    
    - Absorption happens freely when Photon Energy $h\nu \ge E_g$.
        
    - This creates the "Slope" or "Continuum" region on the right side of the graph.
        

#### 3. Excitons (The Peaks below $E_g$)

- **Definition:** A bound state of an electron and a hole, held together by electrostatic attraction (Coulomb force). They are electrically neutral quasiparticles.
    
- **Position on Graph:** They appear at energies **slightly less** than the band gap.
    
    $$E_{\text{exciton}} = E_g - E_{\text{binding}}$$
    
- **The Hydrogen Model (Wannier-Mott Exciton):** Excitons behave like Hydrogen atoms. Their energy levels are quantized ($n=1, 2, 3...$).
    
    $$E_n = E_g - \frac{R^*}{n^2}$$
    
    - $n=1$: The **Ground State** (The first, strongest peak).
        
    - $n=2$: The **First Excited State** (The second, usually smaller peak).
        
    - $R^*$: The effective Rydberg constant (depends on material properties like effective mass and permittivity).
        

#### 4. Fine Structure (Why two peaks?)

If the question asks for "Alternative Explanations" for multiple peaks, use these:

- **Option A (Excited States):** Peak 1 is $n=1$ (1s), Peak 2 is $n=2$ (2s).
    
- **Option B (Valence Band Splitting):** The valence band often has two sub-bands with different curvature (different effective masses):
    
    - **Heavy Hole (HH):** Creates the lower energy exciton.
        
    - **Light Hole (LH):** Creates the higher energy exciton.

### 📝 Quick Visual Identifier

Draw a tiny sketch of the graph on your sheet and label the regions like this:

1. **Region $h\nu < E_g$ (The Peaks):**
    
    - Discrete lines.
        
    - Physics: **Exciton Formation**.
        
    - Electron is "bound" to the hole.
        
2. **Region $h\nu \approx E_g$ (The Valley/Start of Slope):**
    
    - The transition point.
        
    - Physics: **Band Gap Energy**.
        
3. **Region $h\nu > E_g$ (The Rising Slope):**
    
    - Continuous curve.
        
    - Physics: **Band-to-Band Absorption** (Continuum).
        
    - Electron is "free" in the conduction band.
