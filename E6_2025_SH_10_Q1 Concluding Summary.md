## 📝 Cheat Sheet: Effective Mass & Band Theory

### 1. Effective Mass ($m_{eff}$ or $m^*$)

**Concept:** A parameter that replaces the real mass ($m_0$) to account for the crystal lattice's internal forces. It allows you to treat electrons in a solid as "free particles" obeying Newton's Second Law ($F = m^* a$).

**The Formula:**

$$m_{eff} = \frac{\hbar^2}{\frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial k^2}}$$

**How to Read the Graph (Curvature):**

-   **High Curvature (Steep Parabola):**
    
    -   $\frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial k^2}$ is **Large**.
        
    -   $m_{eff}$ is **Small** (Light particle, moves easily).
        
-   **Low Curvature (Flat Parabola):**
    
    -   $\frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial k^2}$ is **Small**.
        
    -   $m_{eff}$ is **Large** (Heavy particle, sluggish).
        
-   **Negative Curvature (Frowning Parabola $\cap$):**
    
    -   $m_{eff}$ is **Negative**.
        
    -   This represents a **Hole** (top of the valence band).

### 3. Key Vocabulary

-   **Dispersion Relation ($E(k)$):** The relationship between energy ($E$) and momentum ($k$). It determines the band structure.
    
-   **Fermi Energy ($E_F$):** The highest energy state occupied by electrons at absolute zero temperature.
    
-   **Conduction Band:** The lowest unfilled band (where electrons move).
    
-   **Valence Band:** The highest filled band (where holes move).
