### **1. Relativistic Kinematics (The Accelerator)**

- **Center-of-Mass Energy ($\sqrt{s}$):**
    
    - The "useful" collision energy.
        
    - _Formula:_ $s \approx 4 E_1 E_2$ (for collider with $E \gg m$).
        
    - _Trap:_ Don't just add energies ($E_1+E_2$) unless you are in the center-of-mass frame already.
        
- **Energy-Momentum Relation:**
    
    - _Formula:_ $E^2 = (pc)^2 + (m c^2)^2$.
        
    - _Approximation:_ If $E \gg m$, then $E \approx pc$ (light speed).
        
- **Bending Radius (Cyclotron Formula):**
    
    - _Formula:_ $p = q B R$.
        
    - _Practical Unit Shortcut:_ $p [\text{GeV}/c] = 0.3 \cdot B [\text{T}] \cdot R [\text{m}]$.
        
- **Synchrotron Radiation:**
    
    - Energy lost per turn when moving in a circle.
        
    - _Scaling:_ $\Delta E \propto \frac{1}{m^4}$.
        
    - _Takeaway:_ Heavy particles (Muons/Protons) lose way less energy than light ones (Electrons).
        

### **2. Particle Detection**

- **Geiger-Müller Counter Regions:**
    
    1. **Ionization:** No amplification. Signal $\propto$ Energy.
        
    2. **Proportional:** Secondary avalanches. Signal $\propto$ Energy (Good for Particle ID).
        
    3. **Geiger-Müller:** Complete saturation. Huge signal, but **no** energy info (Just counting).
        
- **Identifying Particles:**
    
    - _Momentum ($p$):_ Measure curvature in B-field ($R$).
        
    - _Mass ($m$):_ Combine $p$ with **Velocity ($v$)** or **Energy Loss ($dE/dx$)**.
        
        - _Low Energy:_ Use Energy Loss (Bethe-Bloch) or Time-of-Flight.
            
        - _High Energy:_ Use Cherenkov angle.
            

### **3. Radioactivity & Nuclear Physics**

- **Decay Law:**
    
    - _Activity:_ $A(t) = A_0 e^{-\lambda t} = A_0 (1/2)^{t/T_{1/2}}$.
        
    - _Decay Constant:_ $\lambda = \ln 2 / T_{1/2}$.
        
- **Line Width (Heisenberg):**
    
    - _Formula:_ $\Gamma = \hbar / \tau = \hbar \lambda$.
        
    - _Partial Width:_ $\Gamma_{partial} = \Gamma_{total} \times \text{Branching Ratio}$.
        
- **Valley of Stability:**
    
    - _Light Nuclei ($A < 20$):_ $N \approx Z$.
        
    - _Heavy Nuclei ($A > 20$):_ $N > Z$ (Neutron excess needed to glue protons against Coulomb repulsion).
        
    - _Death of Nuclei:_ Fission and Alpha decay occur when Coulomb repulsion overwhelms the Strong Force.
        

### **4. Scattering & Structure**

- **Rosenbluth Formula:** Describes Electron-Proton scattering.
    
    - If the proton were a **point**, Form Factors $G_E = G_M = 1$.
        
    - Since $G_E, G_M$ drop with $Q^2$, the proton has **size/structure**.
        
- **Anomalous Magnetic Moment:**
    
    - $\mu_p \approx 2.79$ (Expected: 1).
        
    - $\mu_n \approx -1.91$ (Expected: 0).
        
    - _Conclusion:_ Neutrons have internal charged currents (Quarks!).
        

### **5. The Golden Rules of Reactions (Conservation)**

When checking if a reaction is allowed, go down this list in order:

1. **Charge ($Q$):** Must balance.
    
2. **Baryon Number ($B$):** Protons/Neutrons $= 1$. Quarks $= 1/3$. Mesons/Leptons $= 0$. Must balance.
    
3. **Lepton Number ($L$):**
    
    - Check families separately: $L_e$, $L_\mu$, $L_\tau$.
        
    - _Remember:_ Neutrinos ($\nu$) are $+1$, Anti-neutrinos ($\bar{\nu}$) are $-1$.
        
4. **Energy:** Parent mass > Daughter masses (for decays).
    
5. **Strangeness ($S$):**
    
    - **Strong/EM Interaction:** $\Delta S = 0$ (Strictly conserved).
        
    - **Weak Interaction:** $\Delta S = 1$ is allowed (Flavor change).
        

### **6. The Quark Model (Mesons)**

- **Quarks:** $u (+\frac{2}{3})$, $d (-\frac{1}{3})$, $s (-\frac{1}{3}, S=-1)$.
    
- **Kaons ($K$):** The lightest strange mesons.
    
    - $K^+ (u\bar{s})$, $K^0 (d\bar{s})$ $\rightarrow S = +1$.
        
    - $K^- (s\bar{u})$, $\bar{K}^0 (s\bar{d})$ $\rightarrow S = -1$.
        
- **Decay Rule:** The lightest strange particle (Kaon) **must** decay via the **Weak Interaction** because it has to turn a strange quark into a non-strange one (flavor change).
