### 🎓 **Solid State Cheat Sheet: Planes & Structures**

#### **1. The Syntax (Brackets Matter!)**

-   **$(h k l)$** = **Specific Plane**. (e.g., The specific face on the right side of the cube).
    
-   **$\{h k l\}$** = **Family of Planes**. (All planes that are symmetrically equivalent/identical).
    
-   **$[u v w]$** = **Direction**. (Vector direction).
    
-   **$<u v w>$** = **Family of Directions**.
    

#### **2. How to Draw a Plane $(h k l)$**

The indices $(h, k, l)$ tell you where the plane cuts the axes.

-   **Rule:** The plane intersects the axes at **$\frac{1}{h}, \frac{1}{k}, \frac{1}{l}$**.
    
-   **Zeros:** If an index is $0$, the intercept is $\frac{1}{0} = \infty$. The plane is **parallel** to that axis.
    
    -   _Example $(110)$:_ Cuts $x$ at 1, $y$ at 1, parallel to $z$ (vertical wall).
        
    -   _Example $(111)$:_ Cuts $x, y, z$ at 1 (corner-cutting triangle).
        

#### **3. Calculating Multiplicity (Counting the Family)**

To find how many planes are in a family $\{hkl\}$ for a **Cubic** system:

1.  **Permute Positions:** How many ways can you rearrange the numbers?
    
2.  **Permute Signs:** How many ways can you flip $+/-$ (only for non-zero numbers)?
    
    -   _Example $\{100\}$:_ 3 positions ($100, 010, 001$) $\times$ 2 signs ($+,-$) = **6 planes**.
        
    -   _Example $\{110\}$:_ 3 positions ($110, 101, 011$) $\times$ 4 signs ($++, --, +-, -+$) = **12 planes**.
        

#### **4. Symmetry Rules (Cubic vs. Tetragonal)**

-   **Cubic ($a=b=c$):** Maximum symmetry. $x, y, z$ are interchangeable.
    
    -   $\{100\}$ includes all 6 faces.
        
-   **Tetragonal ($a=b \neq c$):** The $z$-axis ($c$) is unique (the "odd one out").
    
    -   You **cannot** swap the $l$ index (z-axis) with $h$ or $k$.
        
    -   $\{100\}$ splits into two families:
        
        1.  **Side faces:** $\{100\}$ (4 planes: front, back, left, right).
            
        2.  **Top/Bottom:** $\{001\}$ (2 planes).

#### **6. Stacking Sequences (The Packing Rule)**

How do we stack the hexagonal sheets from the FCC $\{111\}$ plane?

-   **FCC:** Stacking order **ABC ABC...** (Layer 3 is in a unique position).
    
-   **HCP:** Stacking order **AB AB...** (Layer 3 is directly above Layer 1).
    
-   _Note:_ Both have the same maximum Packing Density ($74\%$).
