# 📝 Cheat Sheet: Electron Density & Dimensionality

### 1. The "Master Formula"

The electron density $n_e(E)$ (what you see on the y-axis) is always the product of two functions:

$$n_e(E) = \underbrace{g(E)}_{\text{Density of States}} \times \underbrace{f(E, T)}_{\text{Fermi-Dirac Distribution}}$$

-   **$g(E)$ determines the SHAPE (Low Energy).** It tells you the dimensionality.
    
-   **$f(E, T)$ determines the EDGE (High Energy).** It tells you the temperature.

> -   If $n(E)$ is **flat** $\rightarrow$ **2D**
>     
> -   If $n(E)$ **drops** from high $\rightarrow$ **1D**

### 3. Determining Temperature (Look at the Right Side)

The Fermi-Dirac distribution $f(E, T)$ dictates how the graph ends (the "cutoff").

**Formula:**

$$f(E, T) = \frac{1}{e^{(E - E_F) / k_B T} + 1}$$

> **Exam Tip:**

> -   Sharp corner? $\rightarrow$ **Low Temp (10K)**
>     
> -   Round shoulder? $\rightarrow$ **High Temp (3000K)**

### 4. Summary Table for Quick ID

If you see this combination...

-   **Flat Line + Sharp Drop** = 2D, Low T
    
-   **Flat Line + Smooth Slope** = 2D, High T
    
-   **Falling Curve + Sharp Drop** = 1D, Low T
    
-   **Falling Curve + Smooth Slope** = 1D, High T
    
-   **Rising Curve + Sharp Drop** = 3D, Low T
    
-   **Rising Curve + Smooth Slope** = 3D, High T
