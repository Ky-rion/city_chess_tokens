### 🎓 Particle Physics Cheat Sheet

#### 1. The Golden Rule: Gell-Mann–Nishijima Relation

This connects electric charge to strong interaction quantum numbers.

$$Q = I_3 + \frac{Y}{2}$$

- **$Q$**: Electric Charge
    
- **$I_3$**: 3rd Component of Isospin
    
- **$Y$**: Hypercharge
    

**Useful Rearrangement:**

If you need to find Isospin components (like in part a), flip the formula:

$$I_3 = Q - \frac{Y}{2}$$

---

#### 2. Defining Hypercharge ($Y$)

Hypercharge is the sum of Baryon number and Flavor quantum numbers.

$$Y = A + S + C + B' + T$$

- **$A$ (or $B$):** Baryon Number (Protons/Neutrons/Deltas = $+1$, Mesons/Pions = $0$, Antibaryons = $-1$).
    
- **$S$:** Strangeness (No strange quarks = $0$).
    
- **Note:** For light non-strange baryons (like $p, n, \Delta$), $Y = 1$. For pions, $Y = 0$.
    

---

#### 3. Isospin Rules

**Multiplicity (Counting States):**

If you know the total Isospin $I$, the number of particles in that "family" is:

$$\text{Number of States} = 2I + 1$$

- _Example:_ For $\Delta$, $I=3/2 \rightarrow 2(1.5)+1 = 4$ states (Quartet).
    

**Range of $I_3$:**

Values go from $+I$ to $-I$ in integer steps.

- _Example ($I=3/2$):_ $+3/2, +1/2, -1/2, -3/2$.
    

---

#### 4. Decay Conservation Laws

When checking if a decay is possible (especially strong decays like $\Delta \to N\pi$), check these two "budgets":

1. **Mass/Energy:** $Mass(\text{Parent}) > Mass(\text{Daughters})$.
    
2. **Strong Isospin ($I_3$):** The sum of $I_3$ on the left must equal the sum of $I_3$ on the right.
    
    $$\sum I_{3(\text{parent})} = \sum I_{3(\text{products})}$$
    

---

**Professor's Exam Tip:**

Always check the **interaction type**.

- If the problem mentions "decay after a short time" or "resonance," it is a **Strong Interaction**.
    
- **Strong Interactions** conserve **everything** ($I, I_3, S, Y, Q, A$).
    
- If Strangeness ($S$) changes, or the decay is slow, it is a **Weak Interaction** (which does _not_ conserve Isospin).
