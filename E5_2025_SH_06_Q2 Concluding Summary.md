### **Particle Physics Cheat Sheet: Electron-Proton Scattering**

#### **1. The Players (Four-Vectors)**

-   **Incoming Electron ($p$):** $p = (E/c, \vec{k})$ (Mass neglected)
    
-   **Target Proton ($P$):** $P = (Mc, \vec{0})$ (At rest)
    
-   **Scattered Electron ($p'$):** $p' = (E'/c, \vec{p}')$
    
-   **Photon ($q$):** The momentum transfer. $q = p - p'$
    

#### **2. Fundamental Conservation**

-   **Conservation of Four-Momentum:**
    
    $$p + P = p' + P'$$
    
    _(Incoming + Target = Outgoing Electron + Recoiling System)_
    

---

#### **3. Key Variables & Formulas**

**A. Momentum Transfer ($Q^2$)**

-   **Definition:** "How hard" the collision was.
    
-   **Formula:**
    
    $$Q^2 = -q^2 \approx \frac{2EE'}{c^2}(1 - \cos\theta)$$
    
    _(Valid for both Elastic and Inelastic scattering when electron mass is neglected)_
    

**B. Bjorken Scaling Variable ($x$)**

-   **Definition:** Measures elasticity.
    
-   **Formula:**
    
    $$x = \frac{Q^2}{2 P \cdot q} = \frac{Q^2}{2 Mc (E - E')/c} = \frac{Q^2}{2M(E - E')}$$
    
    _(Note: $E-E'$ is the energy lost by the electron, often called $\nu$)_
    

**C. Invariant Mass ($W$)**

-   **Definition:** The mass of the recoiling system ($P'$).
    
-   **Formula:**
    
    $$W^2 = M^2 + 2M(E - E') - Q^2$$
    
    _(Derived from conservation laws)_

#### **5. Important Constants**

-   **Proton Mass ($M_p$):** $0.938 \text{ GeV}/c^2$
    
-   **Speed of Light ($c$):** Usually set to 1 in "Natural Units," but if you see $c$ in a formula, check your units!
    
-   **Conversion:** $1 \text{ GeV} = 1000 \text{ MeV}$.
