### 📝 Solid State Physics Cheat Sheet: Diamond Structure

#### 1. Structure Identification
*   **Name:** Diamond Structure (*Diamantstruktur*).
*   **Examples:** Carbon (Diamond), Silicon (Si), Germanium (Ge).
*   **Description:** Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) lattice with **half** of the tetrahedral voids filled.
*   **Lattice Type:** Bravais Lattice is **fcc** (*kfz - kubisch flächenzentriert*).
*   **Stacking Sequence:** **ABC ABC** (This creates the fcc lattice).
    *   *(Note: AB AB creates hcp).*

#### 2. The Lattice & Basis (Math Definition)
$$ \text{Structure} = \text{Lattice} + \text{Basis} $$
*   **Lattice Points:** FCC (Corners + Face centers).
*   **Basis:** **2 Atoms** per lattice point.
*   **Basis Coordinates:**
    1.  $(0, 0, 0)$
    2.  $(\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{4})$

#### 3. Counting Atoms ($N$)
Total atoms in the Unit Cell (*Einheitszelle*): **8 Atoms**
*   **Calculation:**
    *   Corners: $8 \times \frac{1}{8} = 1$
    *   Faces: $6 \times \frac{1}{2} = 3$
    *   Interior: $4 \times 1 = 4$
    *   **Total:** $1 + 3 + 4 = 8$

#### 4. Geometry & Hard Spheres
*   **Where do they touch?** Along the **Body Diagonal** (*Raumdiagonale*).
*   **Body Diagonal Length:** $D = \sqrt{3}a$
*   **Nearest Neighbor Distance (Bond Length):** The distance is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the body diagonal.
    $$ 2r = \frac{\sqrt{3}a}{4} $$
*   **Radius ($r$) vs Lattice Constant ($a$):**
    $$ r = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{8}a $$

#### 5. Packing Density (PD / APF)
*   **Formula:**
    $$ PD = \frac{N_{\text{atoms}} \cdot V_{\text{sphere}}}{V_{\text{cell}}} $$
*   **Calculation for Diamond:**
    $$ PD = \frac{8 \cdot \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3}{a^3} $$
*   **Final Value:**
    $$ PD = \frac{\sqrt{3}\pi}{16} \approx \mathbf{0.34} \, (34\%) $$
    *(Comparison: Simple FCC metal is 0.74 or 74%).*

### ⚡ Professor's Exam Tips
1.  **Don't confuse $N$:** If the question asks for the number of lattice points in FCC, it is **4**. If it asks for the number of atoms in a Diamond Unit Cell, it is **8**.
2.  **Look at the diagonal:** If you forget the radius formula, draw a cube, draw the diagonal ($\sqrt{3}a$), and remember the diamond atoms sit at the $1/4$ mark.
3.  **Stacking:** If you see "ABC", think **Cubic** (FCC). If you see "AB", think **Hexagonal** (HCP).
